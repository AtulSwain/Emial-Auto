Getting in — step by step

  1. Open your Otakuva link. Tap Go to Otakuva on the "You're in" page, or Open Otakuva in your access email. It takes you to the Otakuva welcome page.

  2. Choose how to sign in. Tap Sign in with Google for the quickest route, or sign up with your email, a username and a password.

  3. Enter your one-time code. Otakuva emails you a 6-digit code (subject: "123456 is your OTAKUVA code"). Enter it on the site. It expires in 10 minutes.

  4. Set your password. If asked, choose a password so you can sign in again later on any device.

  5. You're in. Pick a username, and you can start exploring.

Can't find the code or your invite? Check Spam and Promotions — Otakuva is new, so Gmail sometimes files us there. Mark it "Not spam" and future emails will land in your inbox.

What to try first

  1. Browse. Monthly rankings, the Top 100, and anime by genre and country.

  2. Review. Write about a show you love and start your climb through the reviewer ranks, from Kōhai to Densetsu.

  3. Connect. Create or join a community around the shows you care about.

  4. Use it like you normally would. On your phone and on a computer, at your own pace. Real habits tell me far more than a checklist.

How to give feedback

You will find rough edges — that's exactly why you're here. Anytime during your 7 days, and especially at the end, I'd love your unfiltered thoughts:

  1. What felt great? The moments you enjoyed or would tell a friend about.

  2. What felt off? Anything confusing, slow, broken or ugly. Screenshots help a lot.

  3. What's missing? The feature that would make Otakuva your go-to anime home.

Easiest way: just reply to any Otakuva email. It comes straight to me, and I read every reply.

A few ground rules

  1. Your link is personal. Please don't share it — it's tied to your invite.

  2. Your access lasts 7 days from the moment you accepted. The exact end date is in your access email.

  3. Stuck? Reply to any of my emails and I'll help you directly.

Your perspective will directly shape what Otakuva becomes at launch. Thank you for being one of the first.
